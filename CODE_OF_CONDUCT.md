# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or career stage.

Project participants are expected to communicate constructively, critique ideas
rather than people, respect privacy, acknowledge uncertainty, and prioritize
scientific integrity. Harassment, discriminatory language, intimidation, and the
publication of others' private information are unacceptable.

Concerns may be reported privately to the project maintainer through the contact
information on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled
as confidentially as practicable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the safety
or integrity of the community.
